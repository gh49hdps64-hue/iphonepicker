Which iPhone should you switch to?

Each person gets a $700 phone credit on T-Mobile's T-Mix Standard plan. No trade-in needed: keep or sell your old phone. Here's what the credit buys and how each phone compares to the one you have now.

The plan: T-Mix Standard

$25 per person, per month

With AutoPay, plus taxes and fees.

What you get

  • Unlimited talk, text and data in the US
  • 50GB of fast "premium" data a month
  • Unlimited texting from 215+ countries
  • Talk, text and basic data in Canada and Mexico

What you don't get

  • Fast hotspot (it works, but only at slow 3G speed)
  • HD video (streams at 480p, standard definition)
  • Fast data abroad (Canada and Mexico data is very slow)
  • Priority after 50GB (data can slow down when the network is busy)

Fine print and how the credit works
  • Phone cost is what's left after the $700 credit, spread over 36 months, added to your $25 plan. All three phones start with 256GB of storage.
  • The credit comes as 36 monthly bill credits that pay down the phone. If you cancel early or pay the phone off, the remaining credits stop and you owe the rest.
  • The phone credit uses that line's Picks for the 36 months, so that line won't also get a free perk like Netflix.
  • Due at purchase: sales tax on the full phone price and a $35 device connection charge per line.
  • Full prices at T-Mobile: iPhone 17e $699.99 (256GB) or $899.99 (512GB); iPhone 17 $929.99 or $1,129.99; iPhone 18 Pro $1,199.99, $1,399.99 (512GB), $1,799.99 (1TB) or $2,399.99 (2TB). The $700 credit is the same at every size.
  • No trade-in is required for the credit.
  • Plan price is with AutoPay ($10/line more without), before taxes and fees. Confirm everything at the store before you sign.
